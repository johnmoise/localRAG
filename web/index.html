          Faster citation lookups

          localRAG looks up authors, years and page numbers for your documents on Crossref. With an email address, Crossref puts the requests in its polite pool (up to 10 lookups a second instead of 5, and 3 title searches instead of 1). Crossref only uses it to contact you if your requests cause problems.

          Stored on this Mac only (in ~/.config/localrag, private to your user), never in a workspace, and sent only to Crossref. You can change it later in Settings.
